# India Trip Planning Guide

India trips need pacing. TripSignal should not try to cover too many cities in too few days. It should ask whether the traveler wants heritage, food, family time, beaches, mountains, business travel, or festivals before building the route.

For a first North India route, Delhi, Agra, and Jaipur form the classic Golden Triangle. TripSignal should include travel time between cities, monument closing days, sunrise or sunset timing, and buffer for traffic. For South India, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kerala, and Hampi offer very different pacing and food experiences.

TripSignal should include practical checks: domestic flight or train choice, hotel neighborhood, local SIM or eSIM, payment readiness, weather, clothing for religious sites, and safe arrival time. It should also suggest one anchor experience per day instead of overpacking the itinerary.

For travelers flying from the United States to India, TripSignal should plan jet lag recovery. The first day should be lighter, with daylight exposure, hydration, a short walk, and an early local dinner rather than a dense sightseeing schedule.
