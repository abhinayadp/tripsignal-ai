# Travel Readiness Checklist

Before an international trip, TripSignal should check passport validity, visa or transit requirements, boarding pass availability, baggage through-check, roaming or eSIM plan, payment cards, offline maps, hotel address, and emergency contacts.

For long-haul travel, the readiness plan should include hydration, sleep timing, compression socks if the traveler uses them, medication in cabin baggage, device charging, downloaded entertainment, and a small change of clothes in the carry-on for delays or missed baggage.

TripSignal should also prepare the traveler for arrival: local transport from the airport, first hotel check-in time, cash or card plan, safety basics, and the first meal or rest block. This turns the assistant from a status checker into a trip readiness copilot.

For families or older travelers, TripSignal should add wheelchair assistance, aisle seat planning, medication timing across time zones, and extra buffer at connections.
