# Connection Risk Guide

A connection is risky when the scheduled layover is close to the airport minimum connection time, when the inbound flight is already delayed, when the traveler must change terminals, or when weather is affecting the hub.

TripSignal uses three risk bands:
- Low risk: scheduled connection is more than 90 minutes and inbound status is on time.
- Medium risk: scheduled connection is 45 to 90 minutes, or inbound delay is 15 to 45 minutes.
- High risk: scheduled connection is under 45 minutes, inbound delay is greater than the available buffer, or the itinerary requires a terminal change at a large hub.

For high-risk connections, the traveler should not wait until landing to act. The recommended action is to message or call the carrier during the first flight, ask for a protected backup flight, and identify the last reasonable departure to the final destination.

TripSignal should avoid saying a traveler will definitely misconnect unless the projected arrival is after the connecting flight's boarding cutoff. It should say the connection is at risk and give the next decision point.
