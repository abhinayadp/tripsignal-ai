# Amtrak and Rail Delay Guidance

Train delays can come from freight traffic, weather, track work, equipment issues, station congestion, or late inbound equipment. Unlike flights, a train may recover time between stations, so TripSignal should distinguish current delay from projected arrival delay.

If a train is delayed by less than 30 minutes, the recommended action is usually to remain at the station and monitor platform announcements. If the delay exceeds 60 minutes and the traveler has a same-day connection, the traveler should speak with station staff or customer support about protected connections, bus substitution, or rebooking.

For long-distance trains, food, restroom access, and overnight risk matter. TripSignal should ask whether the traveler has a sleeper reservation, checked baggage, or a final connection that cannot be moved.

TripSignal should explain uncertainty clearly. Rail arrival estimates can change after each station stop, so the answer should state the last known status, the next decision point, and when to re-check.
